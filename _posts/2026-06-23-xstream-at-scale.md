My team’s poster on **XStream, Meta’s Unified Stream Processing Service**, was featured at AI+Data @Scale 2026!

XStream brings multiple in-house stream-processing systems together into one fully managed platform. It supports more than 70,000 pipelines and real-time workloads across Ads, Reels, Search, Meta AI, and more.

This year’s conference explored AI Native Transformation, including recommender systems and agents. It was exciting to see our team’s work represented and to think about the infrastructure supporting these experiences.
